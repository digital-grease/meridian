Title: April 2026 &mdash; hedge density creeps up on political axis
Date: 2026-04-30
Week:
Type: monthly
Axes: political, historical-contested
Summary: Across all three observed providers, hedge-density on political-axis prompts rose roughly 8&ndash;12% during April. Refusal behavior is otherwise stable. Full discussion of what may have driven it and what to watch for in May.

## The month in one sentence

Hedge-density on political-axis prompts ticked up across all three
providers this month, most visibly in the final two weeks. Nothing else
moved meaningfully.

## What we observed

- **Weeks 14 and 15**: flat relative to March.
- **Weeks 16 and 17**: 8&ndash;12% rise in hedge-density on political
  prompts, holding above the confidence interval for both weeks.
- No corresponding rise in refusal-rate on the same prompts. Models are
  still answering; they are answering with more caveat.

## Possible drivers

Attribution is hard. Possibilities:

- A provider-side policy adjustment that affects framing guidance. No
  provider has published a changelog entry in this window.
- A silent model update. Our detection on the neutral-control axis does
  not flag one, which is evidence against an across-the-board update
  but not dispositive.
- A genuine training-data shift from recent discourse. If April&rsquo;s
  political news had more contested framing, RLHF pipelines may have
  inherited some of that caution.

We do not yet have enough evidence to attribute. The next step is
running the held-out political-axis prompts (not yet published) to
confirm the public-corpus signal is not a benchmark-targeting artifact.

## What to watch in May

- Does the hedge-density rise continue or reverse?
- Do any providers publish a policy or model update that could correlate?
- Does the held-out split show the same pattern?
