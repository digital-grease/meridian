No data: {{ week.week_id }}

ISO week {{ week.week_id }} has no snapshot. The scheduled run produced no samples for any model, so there are no metrics, no raw responses, and no manifest for this week.

Why this page exists

This week sits between weeks that do have data. A longitudinal record whose holes are invisible is worse than one with holes, so the week keeps its own URL and its own column in every model × week table on this site. Time-series charts break the line here rather than joining the weeks on either side, and aggregate statistics exclude the week rather than imputing it.

What caused this particular gap, and how long it took us to notice, is written up under known data gaps.

Not backfilled

The week was not re-sampled afterwards and will not be. A run made later would carry today’s model behaviour under this week’s label, which is precisely the substitution this record exists to make visible. The week stays empty.
